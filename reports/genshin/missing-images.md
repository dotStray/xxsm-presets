# genshin: characters with no portrait

2 characters have no picture. The app shows their initials instead. To add one, put a picture in `manual/genshin/images/` named after the character, like `XilonenCoat.png`.

- **XilonenCoat** — Xilonen Coat: no picture in any source
- **XilonenSkates** — Xilonen Skates: no picture in any source
