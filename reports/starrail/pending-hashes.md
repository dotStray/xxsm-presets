# starrail: characters waiting for hashes

0 characters in the pack have no hashes yet. They are still usable: they appear in the grid, take mods filed by hand or by name, and offer *Learn hashes from a mod*. A shrinking list is the pipeline working; a growing one means an upstream hash repository has gone quiet.

To add hashes yourself, put a file in `manual/starrail/hashes/` named after the character.
