# starrail: characters with no portrait

2 characters have no picture. The app shows their initials instead. To add one, put a picture in `manual/starrail/images/` named after the character, like `CaelusVigor.png`.

- **CaelusVigor** — Caelus Vigor: no picture in any source
- **StelleVigor** — Stelle Vigor: no picture in any source
